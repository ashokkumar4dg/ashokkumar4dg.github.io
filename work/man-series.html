Man Series

UI/UX Design — Shopify E-commerce
Visit Live Site →
Role Graphic & UI/UX Designer
Client Man Series (D2C Skincare)
Timeline May 2026 — Present
Deliverables Shopify Store, UI/UX, Visual Direction
The Brief

As part of my role at Man Series, I designed and improved the brand's Shopify e-commerce experience, translating ingredient-led product positioning into clear, conversion-focused visual communication.

The Approach

Working directly with the founders on creative direction, I helped shape a modern digital experience for the brand. We focused on establishing clean visual hierarchies, ingredient-led product storytelling, and streamlined collection browsing tailored for mobile-first skincare shoppers.

Every section was structured to balance scientific credibility with clean, minimalist aesthetics—ensuring ingredients, benefits, and usage routines are immediately understandable before checkout.

The Work

Desktop, tablet, and mobile responsive storefront experiences designed for the live Man Series D2C skincare store.

The Result

The live storefront delivers an intuitive, elevated shopping journey supporting brand growth, customer trust, and engagement across the competitive D2C skincare market.

Monsoon Memories

Editorial & Book Cover Design
Commission a Cover →
Role Book Cover & Editorial Designer
Client Independent Novel Publication
Timeline 2025
Deliverables Front Cover, Spine, Back Jacket, Print Specs
The Brief

The author required an evocative, emotionally resonant cover design that stands out in both digital bookstore thumbnails and physical bookstore shelves, encapsulating the bittersweet themes of nostalgia and monsoon memories.

The Approach

I crafted an atmospheric illustration paired with refined, classic editorial typography. The spine and back jacket were precisely calibrated to print production specifications, ensuring perfect bleeds, barcode placement, and author bio readability.

The Work

Full cover jacket layout featuring front cover art, typography lockup, spine hierarchy, and blurb formatting.

The Result

A market-ready, professional cover design that achieved immediate praise from readers and reviewers, boosting pre-order interest and marketplace visibility.

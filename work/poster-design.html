Swiss Typographic Posters

Print & Grid Systems

Role Typographic & Print Designer
Client Exhibitions & Cultural Events
Timeline 2025
Deliverables Large Format Prints, Grid Layouts, Vector Art
The Brief

Cultural and architectural gatherings require visual communication that conveys order, sophistication, and modernist clarity. The objective was to design a series of Swiss-inspired typographic posters driven by mathematical grid alignment.

The Approach

Applying strict baseline grids, asymmetrical balance, and high-contrast typographic scaling, I explored the tension between negative space and structured geometric elements. Monospaced and grotesque typefaces were utilized to ground the aesthetic in modernist principles.

The Work

High-resolution poster series showcasing typographic hierarchy, diagonal rhythm, and geometric grid precision.

The Result

An impactful print series featured in local exhibitions and digital design galleries, celebrated for its disciplined execution and timeless aesthetic.

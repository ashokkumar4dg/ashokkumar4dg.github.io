Premium Invitation Layouts

Print & Stationery
Commission Stationery →
Role Stationery & Print Designer
Client Private Clients & Luxury Events
Timeline 2025
Deliverables Event Suites, Foil Stamp Prep, Digital Cards
The Brief

Bespoke events and weddings require tactile, elegant invitations that set an unforgettable tone before the celebration begins. The challenge was to blend timeless cultural motifs with modern editorial sophistication.

The Approach

Focusing on high-touch printing methods (foil stamping, debossing, heavy cotton paper stocks), I created bespoke typography lockups framed by minimalist ornamental borders. The suite was designed with coordinated RSVP cards, itinerary inserts, and envelope seals.

The Work

Featured luxury invitation suite detailing vector ornament curves, typography composition, and print hierarchy.

The Result

Delivered an unforgettable tangible keepsake for hosts and guests, praised for its meticulous print craftsmanship and understated luxury.

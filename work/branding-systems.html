Brand Identity & Visual Systems
Inquire for Branding →
Role Lead Brand Identity Designer
Client Modern Startups & Creators
Timeline 2025 — 2026
Deliverables Logo Marks, Brand Guidelines, Typography
The Brief

Startups and creative enterprises frequently struggle with disjointed visual assets across digital touchpoints. The goal was to build comprehensive, modular brand systems that establish immediate recognition, elevate market positioning, and scale effortlessly from mobile app icons to large format print.

The Approach

Starting with core brand values and competitive differentiation, I developed geometric logo marks constructed on mathematical grids. Each system includes rigorous typographic rules, high-contrast monochrome and primary palettes, and flexible mark variations for favicons, headers, and social avatars.

The Work

Comprehensive brand identity system showcase detailing logo geometry, stationery applications, and responsive lockups.

The Result

A cohesive, memorable brand presence that delivers consistency across digital platforms and physical packaging, establishing credibility and clear brand recall.

Marketing & Social Creatives
Start a Campaign →
Role Visual Designer & Content Creator
Client D2C & E-commerce Brands
Timeline 2025 — 2026
Deliverables Instagram Carousels, Ad Creatives, Story Layouts
The Brief

Modern feed consumption requires visual creative that captures immediate attention within milliseconds. The objective was to design scroll-stopping marketing assets and educational carousels that drive product consideration and click-through rates.

The Approach

Combining bold typographic focal points, clean product cutouts, and disciplined whitespace, I established modular ad templates that allow rapid campaign iteration. Content was structured into digestible visual hooks and clear calls-to-action.

The Work

Featured creative campaign showcasing multi-slide carousel storytelling, typographic hierarchy, and conversion-oriented layouts.

The Result

Consistent visual identity and enhanced engagement across organic feeds and paid social ad channels, elevating brand perception and audience retention.

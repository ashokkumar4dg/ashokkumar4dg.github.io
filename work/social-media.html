03 / Visual Communication

Social Media Creative Campaign

High-engagement carousel templates, promotional assets, and brand storytelling.

Discuss a Campaign
Role Creative & Performance Designer
Client D2C & Education Brands
Timeline 2025 — 2026
Deliverables Ad Creatives, Feed Templates, Motion Graphics

The Brief

Social feeds are overcrowded with homogenous templates that fail to stop scrolling. The objective was to design distinctive, narrative-driven carousel graphics and promotional assets that maintain strict brand standards while driving measurable engagement.

The Approach

I combined editorial typography with bold color blocking and product-focused compositions. Carousels were architected with progressive visual disclosure—each slide delivering one clear message leading naturally to the call to action.

The Work

Curated selection of performance marketing creatives, educational carousel assets, and seasonal campaign posters.

The Result

Significantly higher click-through rates and save metrics compared to previous benchmarks, establishing a repeatable visual system for continuous publishing.
